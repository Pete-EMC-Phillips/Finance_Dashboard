Illustrative only — not financial advice. Assumes constant monthly compounding, a constant annual pay rise, and steady contribution rates between scheduled changes. The UK pension Annual Allowance is currently £60,000/tax year (2026/27) across all your pensions combined; this tool checks your rolling 12-month employee + employer contributions against it as a rough guide only — it does not account for the tapered allowance, Money Purchase Annual Allowance, or carry forward. The cash journey chart assumes withdrawals are taken at the start of each year, each scenario's remaining pot keeps growing at its own growth rate, and State/DB pension income only counts from the ages you set. The "required pot" figure is a rough 4%-rule-style target for the income gap above your State + DB pensions — it's a static reference, not part of the year-by-year cash journey simulation. It all ignores tax, inflation, and State Pension age changes.
